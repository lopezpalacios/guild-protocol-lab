Who pays, and why it matters

The first button is a sponsor: the lab's operator key sends the transaction and pays the gas, on your behalf, into your address. You sign nothing and spend nothing.

That is what a paymaster is — a business decision dressed as a protocol feature. Someone is paying for you, and someone is deciding when to stop. The second button is what it looks like when the subsidy is gone.
